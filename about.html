About DAGA

Discover Around Go Again

Who We Are

DAGA is a Smart Local Tourism Business Portal designed to connect tourists with local tourism destinations, businesses and experiences, with a focus on Dakshina Kannada.

Our Aim

Our aim is to make local tourism discovery easier while helping local businesses present their services to visitors.

What DAGA Offers

  • Explore local destinations.
  • Discover homestays, guides and local services.
  • Plan trips based on interests and budget.
  • Send booking requests to local businesses.
  • Help local businesses showcase their offerings.

Our Focus

DAGA focuses on supporting community-based tourism and improving the visibility of local tourism businesses.
